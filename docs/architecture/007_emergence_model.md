# Emergence Model

## Purpose

Zoryn does not primarily forecast hazards.

It forecasts the emergence of organized behaviour within the Earth system.

Hazards are manifestations of emergence.

The objective is to detect emergence before it becomes obvious.

---

# Philosophy

The Earth is a complex adaptive system.

Complex systems produce behaviour that cannot always be understood by studying individual components.

New behaviour emerges through interaction.

Zoryn continuously searches for these emerging behaviours.

---

# Definition

Emergence is the appearance of new system behaviour resulting from interactions among processes.

The behaviour is not explicitly programmed.

It is discovered.

---

# Hierarchy

Reality

↓

Processes

↓

Interactions

↓

Changes

↓

Facts

↓

Projections

↓

Emergent Behaviour

↓

Decisions

Emergence is therefore a property of interacting processes.

---

# Characteristics

Emergent behaviour is often

unexpected,

nonlinear,

multi-scale,

adaptive,

self-organizing,

temporally evolving.

---

# Emergence Indicators

Early emergence may appear as

weak correlations,

new interaction pathways,

persistent anomalies,

increasing synchronization,

unexpected feedback,

changing process influence,

novel trajectories.

None of these alone indicate emergence.

Their accumulation does.

---

# Compound Events

Compound events are examples of emergence.

Examples include

Drought followed by wildfire.

Heat combined with energy failure.

Flooding combined with disease.

Conflict combined with food insecurity.

The combined behaviour is more important than the individual hazards.

---

# Cascades

Emergence frequently develops through cascades.

Small local changes

↓

Process interactions

↓

Regional organization

↓

Large-scale behaviour

The objective is to recognize the cascade while it is still forming.

---

# Novelty

Not every emergent behaviour has historical precedent.

Zoryn therefore searches for

known emergence,

rare emergence,

and entirely novel emergence.

Novel emergence is expected.

---

# Stability

Emergence may

stabilize,

oscillate,

collapse,

or disappear.

These outcomes are continuously learned.

---

# Early Warning

Early warning becomes

detecting organized behaviour

before it produces measurable impacts.

This shifts warning from hazard detection to emergence detection.

---

# Design Principles

1. Emergence results from interacting processes.
2. Hazards are manifestations of emergence.
3. Emergence is detected, not predefined.
4. Weak signals matter.
5. Interactions matter more than isolated observations.
6. Novel emergence is expected.
7. Early warning targets emergence, not hazards.
8. Every new fact refines the understanding of emergence.
