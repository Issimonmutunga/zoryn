# Process Model

## Purpose

Processes are the mechanisms that produce change.

Objects do not change by themselves.

Changes do not occur spontaneously.

Every change is generated by one or more processes.

The objective of Zoryn is to discover, learn and continuously refine
these processes.

---

# Philosophy

Processes are the hidden machinery of the Earth.

Facts are evidence.

Changes are manifestations.

Processes are explanations.

---

# Definition

A process is a continuously operating mechanism that transforms one
state of the Earth into another.

Processes may be

Physical

Biological

Human

Infrastructure

Knowledge

Unknown

# Unknown Processes

Not every process is known.

Some processes are hidden.

Some processes emerge only after long observation.

One objective of Zoryn is discovering previously unknown processes.

---

# Process Graph

Processes interact.

Precipitation

↓

Soil Moisture Recharge

↓

Vegetation Growth

↓

Crop Production

↓

Food Availability

↓

Population Health

Processes therefore form networks rather than chains.

---

# Process Strength

Every process possesses

Magnitude

Rate

Persistence

Direction

Uncertainty

Evidence

These evolve continuously.

---

# Process Discovery

Rather than learning hazards,

Zoryn learns

which processes are active,

which processes are strengthening,

which processes are weakening,

which processes are emerging,

and which processes have never been observed before.

---

# Process Interaction

Processes may

reinforce

suppress

delay

accelerate

redirect

replace

other processes.

These relationships evolve continuously.

---

# Process Memory

The system remembers

how processes behaved,

when they appeared,

what they influenced,

how they interacted,

how they eventually evolved.

---

# Forecasting

Forecasting becomes

future process evolution

rather than

future variable prediction.

Variables become observable consequences of processes.

---

# Design Principles

1. Processes generate changes.
2. Changes generate facts.
3. Facts generate projections.
4. Processes may be discovered.
5. Unknown processes are expected.
6. Process interactions are continually learned.
7. Forecasts project future process evolution.
8. Early warning is early process detection.
