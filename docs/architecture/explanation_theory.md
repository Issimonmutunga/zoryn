# Explanation Theory

## Purpose

The purpose of Zoryn is not merely to predict future behaviour.

Its primary objective is to explain the Earth.

Predictions become consequences of explanation.

---

# Philosophy

Every observation deserves an explanation.

Every explanation must be supported by evidence.

Every explanation remains provisional.

Better evidence produces better explanations.

---

# Definition

An explanation is a coherent description of the processes that
produced an observed sequence of facts.

Explanations describe mechanisms.

They do not merely describe correlations.

---

# Structure

Every explanation contains

Observed Facts

↓

Supporting Evidence

↓

Processes

↓

Interactions

↓

Emergent Behaviour

↓

Observed Outcome

An explanation therefore forms a causal chain.

---

# Multiple Explanations

More than one explanation may exist.

Competing explanations are expected.

Zoryn evaluates competing explanations continuously.

---

# Explanation Quality

An explanation improves when it

explains more observations,

requires fewer assumptions,

predicts future behaviour,

remains consistent across regions,

remains consistent through time,

survives contradictory evidence.

---

# Simplicity

Among explanations with equal explanatory power,

the simpler explanation is preferred.

Complexity alone is not evidence.

---

# Incomplete Explanations

Incomplete explanations are acceptable.

Unknown mechanisms are explicitly represented.

Missing knowledge is never hidden.

---

# Revision

Every explanation remains revisable.

New evidence may

strengthen,

weaken,

replace,

merge,

or divide

existing explanations.

---

# Discovery

When no satisfactory explanation exists,

Zoryn generates candidate processes.

These remain hypotheses until validated.

Failure to explain is therefore productive.

---

# Forecasting

Forecasts inherit confidence from explanations.

Weak explanations produce uncertain forecasts.

Strong explanations produce reliable forecasts.

---

# Scientific Principle

Prediction without explanation is useful.

Explanation with prediction is understanding.

Understanding is the primary objective.

---

# Design Principles

1. Explanations describe mechanisms.
2. Evidence precedes explanation.
3. Competing explanations are expected.
4. Simplicity is preferred.
5. Unknown mechanisms remain explicit.
6. Explanations evolve continuously.
7. Forecasts inherit explanatory confidence.
8. Understanding is the objective.
