# Novelty Theory

## Purpose

The objective of Zoryn is not merely to recognize known behaviour.

Its objective is to discover behaviour that has never been explicitly
defined before.

Novelty is therefore a first-class concept.

---

# Philosophy

The Earth continuously produces new behaviour.

Some novelty is noise.

Some novelty is measurement error.

Some novelty represents genuinely new Earth system dynamics.

Zoryn must distinguish between them.

---

# Definition

Novelty is behaviour that cannot be adequately explained by the
current process model.

Novelty is evidence that the current understanding of the Earth is
incomplete.

---

# Sources of Novelty

Novelty may arise from

new observations,

new interactions,

new processes,

new causal pathways,

new combinations of existing processes,

or entirely unknown mechanisms.

---

# Levels of Novelty

Level 1

Unexpected observation.

Level 2

Unexpected sequence of changes.

Level 3

Unexpected interaction between known processes.

Level 4

Previously unknown process.

Level 5

Previously unknown system behaviour.

Novelty exists on a continuum.

---

# Evidence

Novelty increases when

independent evidence agrees,

multiple regions exhibit similar behaviour,

the behaviour persists,

the behaviour predicts future observations,

existing process models repeatedly fail.

---

# False Novelty

Novelty may be caused by

sensor error,

processing error,

missing observations,

incorrect assumptions,

poor models.

These possibilities must always be evaluated.

---

# Discovery

Novelty initiates learning.

When sufficient evidence accumulates,

candidate processes are created,

validated,

refined,

or rejected.

---

# Scientific Principle

Failure of explanation is valuable.

An unexplained observation is not an error.

It is an opportunity to improve understanding.

---

# Early Warning

Novelty often precedes impact.

The earliest detectable novelty may provide the earliest possible
warning.

The objective is therefore

not merely anomaly detection,

but meaningful novelty detection.

---

# Design Principles

1. Novelty drives learning.
2. Unknown behaviour is expected.
3. Explanations compete.
4. Evidence determines confidence.
5. Discovery never stops.
6. Novelty improves process models.
7. Scientific uncertainty is preserved.
8. Learning is continuous.
