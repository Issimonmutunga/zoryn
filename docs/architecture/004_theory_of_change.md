# Theory of Change

## Purpose

Zoryn is not a state engine.

Zoryn is a change engine.

The purpose of Zoryn is to continuously learn how change propagates through the Earth system.

Rather than asking

"What is happening?"

Zoryn asks

"What changed?"

"Why?"

"What changed because of it?"

"What is likely to change next?"

---

# Philosophy

Everything is change.

States are merely snapshots of continuous change.

Facts record change.

Knowledge describes change.

Forecasts extend change into the future.

---

# Change

A change is the transition of one or more ontology concepts over time.

Examples

Rainfall increased.

River level decreased.

Vegetation recovered.

Reservoir emptied.

Migration accelerated.

Food insecurity expanded.

Knowledge improved.

Everything entering Zoryn ultimately becomes a change.

---

# Sources of Change

Changes originate from

Earth processes

Living processes

Human processes

Infrastructure processes

Knowledge processes

Unknown processes

Unknown causes are acceptable.

---

# Change Graph

Rather than constructing a graph of objects,

Zoryn constructs a graph of changes.

Every change may influence other changes.

Example

Rainfall Deficit

↓

Soil Moisture Decline

↓

Vegetation Stress

↓

Crop Failure

↓

Food Insecurity

↓

Migration

↓

Economic Pressure

Each node represents change.

Edges represent influence.

---

# Causality

Every change may

cause

amplify

suppress

delay

accelerate

redirect

another change.

These relationships evolve through observation.

They are not fixed.

---

# Cascades

Changes rarely occur alone.

They form cascades.

Small changes may eventually become large impacts.

Zoryn continuously searches for

emerging cascades

repeating cascades

unknown cascades

---

# Stability

Some systems resist change.

Others amplify it.

Zoryn learns

stable systems

unstable systems

resilient systems

fragile systems

These are properties of change.

---

# Tipping Points

Certain combinations of changes produce irreversible transitions.

Examples

Forest collapse

Reservoir failure

Famine

Species loss

Infrastructure failure

Zoryn continuously estimates proximity to tipping points.

---

# Precursors

Large changes begin as small changes.

The objective of early warning is to discover

the earliest detectable precursor.

Not necessarily the hazard.

---

# Learning

Every new fact answers

Did something change?

How much?

How fast?

Why?

What followed?

Learning therefore becomes continual refinement of change propagation.

---

# Forecasting

Forecasting is future change projection.

Rather than predicting variables,

Zoryn predicts evolving change networks.

---

# Design Principles

1. Change is the primary object of study.
2. States are temporary observations.
3. Causality is learned continuously.
4. Small changes matter.
5. Cascades are fundamental.
6. Tipping points emerge from accumulated change.
7. Forecasts project future change.
8. Every explanation is a change pathway.
