# Process Discovery

## Purpose

Zoryn does not contain a fixed catalogue of Earth processes.

Instead, it continuously discovers, validates and refines them.

Process discovery is continual.

It never finishes.

---

# Philosophy

Unknown processes exist.

Unknown interactions exist.

Unknown causal pathways exist.

The objective of Zoryn is to discover them before they become widely recognized.

---

# Evidence

A process cannot be observed directly.

Only its consequences can be observed.

Process discovery therefore begins with evidence.

Evidence accumulates through facts.

---

# Discovery Cycle

Evidence

↓

Repeated Changes

↓

Emerging Pattern

↓

Candidate Process

↓

Validation

↓

Known Process

↓

Continuous Refinement

Discovery never ends.

---

# Candidate Processes

Every candidate process represents a possible explanation.

Candidate processes are hypotheses.

They remain provisional until sufficient evidence exists.

---

# Validation

A candidate process gains confidence when

it repeatedly explains observations,

it predicts future changes,

it remains consistent across regions,

it remains consistent across time.

Validation is continuous.

---

# Refinement

Processes evolve.

Their behaviour changes.

Their relationships change.

Their influence changes.

Zoryn therefore updates process definitions continuously.

---

# Process Relationships

Processes rarely operate independently.

Discovery therefore searches for

supporting processes,

competing processes,

hidden processes,

feedback processes,

compound processes.

---

# Unknown Processes

Unknown processes are expected.

Failure to explain observations is evidence that

the current process model is incomplete.

Unknown behaviour becomes an opportunity for discovery.

---

# Discovery Confidence

Every discovered process possesses

confidence,

supporting evidence,

temporal stability,

spatial consistency,

predictive usefulness,

scientific maturity.

These evolve over time.

---

# Continuous Learning

Every incoming fact may

strengthen

weaken

split

merge

replace

or create

a process.

Learning therefore never stops.

---

# Design Principles

1. Processes are inferred.
2. Evidence precedes explanation.
3. Discovery is continual.
4. Validation is evidence-driven.
5. Unknown processes are valuable.
6. Processes evolve.
7. Discovery improves forecasting.
8. Discovery improves understanding.
