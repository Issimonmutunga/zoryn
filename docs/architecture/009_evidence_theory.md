# Evidence Theory

## Purpose

Evidence is the foundation of scientific reasoning within Zoryn.

Facts become useful only when supported by evidence.

Knowledge is accumulated evidence.

Confidence is accumulated evidence.

Discovery is accumulated evidence.

---

# Philosophy

No conclusion exists without evidence.

No evidence exists without facts.

No fact exists without provenance.

Evidence therefore forms the foundation of all reasoning.

---

# Definition

Evidence is any collection of facts that increases or decreases
confidence in an explanation.

Evidence is not binary.

Evidence accumulates.

Evidence evolves.

---

# Evidence Graph

Evidence forms a directed graph.

Fact

↓

Evidence

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Process

↓

Emergence

Every conclusion is therefore traceable back to observations.

---

# Supporting Evidence

Evidence may

support,

strengthen,

confirm,

or reinforce

an explanation.

Supporting evidence increases confidence.

---

# Contradictory Evidence

Evidence may also

contradict,

weaken,

or invalidate

an explanation.

Contradictory evidence is equally valuable.

Scientific progress requires both.

---

# Independent Evidence

Evidence is stronger when obtained from independent sources.

Examples

Satellite observations

Ground stations

Hydrological models

Human observations

Independent agreement increases confidence.

---

# Temporal Evidence

Evidence accumulates through time.

Persistent evidence is generally stronger than isolated evidence.

Long-lived explanations become increasingly reliable.

---

# Spatial Evidence

Evidence also accumulates through space.

An explanation repeatedly observed across different regions
becomes increasingly general.

---

# Evidence Quality

Every evidence source possesses

uncertainty,

accuracy,

precision,

coverage,

timeliness,

provenance.

These properties influence confidence.

---

# Evidence Lifecycle

Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Explanation

↓

Validation

↓

Knowledge

↓

Continuous Revision

Knowledge therefore remains provisional.

---

# Scientific Principle

Absence of evidence

is not

evidence of absence.

Uncertainty must always be preserved.

---

# Design Principles

1. Every conclusion requires evidence.
2. Evidence accumulates continuously.
3. Contradictory evidence is valuable.
4. Provenance is mandatory.
5. Evidence quality influences confidence.
6. Knowledge remains revisable.
7. Every explanation is traceable.
8. Evidence drives scientific discovery.
