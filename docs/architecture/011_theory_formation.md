# Theory Formation

## Purpose

The purpose of Zoryn is not merely to accumulate facts.

Its purpose is to continuously construct increasingly accurate
theories describing how the Earth functions.

Theories are the highest level of knowledge within the system.

---

# Philosophy

Facts describe observations.

Evidence supports explanations.

Explanations describe processes.

Theories organize explanations.

Scientific understanding therefore progresses from observations
towards increasingly coherent theories.

---

# Definition

A theory is a coherent collection of validated explanations that
together describe the behaviour of part of the Earth system.

A theory explains

what happens,

why it happens,

when it happens,

where it happens,

under which conditions it changes.

---

↓

No theory is permanent.

---

# Competing Theories

Multiple theories may explain the same observations.

Competing theories are expected.

Zoryn continuously evaluates them using incoming evidence.

---

# Theory Quality

A theory improves when it

explains more evidence,

predicts future observations,

remains internally consistent,

remains externally consistent,

requires fewer assumptions,

discovers previously unknown behaviour.

---

# Theory Evolution

New evidence may

strengthen,

weaken,

merge,

split,

replace,

or reject

existing theories.

Theory evolution never stops.

---

# Unknown Theory

Some observations cannot yet be explained.

Rather than forcing an explanation,

Zoryn records

Unknown Theory

and waits for additional evidence.

Scientific uncertainty is preserved.

---

---

# Discovery

The objective is not only to improve existing theories.

The objective is also to discover entirely new theories that have
never been explicitly proposed by humans.

---

# Design Principles

1. Theories organize explanations.
2. Theories compete continuously.
3. Better evidence improves theories.
4. Unknown theories are expected.
5. Theory evolution never stops.
6. Prediction follows theory.
7. Scientific uncertainty is preserved.
8. Discovery is continuous.
