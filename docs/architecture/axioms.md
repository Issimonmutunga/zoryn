# Zoryn Axioms

## Axiom 1

Facts are the only source of truth.

Everything else is derived.

---

## Axiom 2

Facts are immutable.

Corrections create new facts.

Nothing is edited.

Nothing is deleted.

---

## Axiom 3

Time is intrinsic.

Every fact exists within time.

No timeless knowledge exists.

---

## Axiom 4

Knowledge is projection.

Graphs, memories, statistics and forecasts are different
interpretations of the same facts.

---

## Axiom 5

Reasoning never changes facts.

Reasoning produces new facts.

---

## Axiom 6

Prediction is future projection.

Forecasting is projecting the fact stream beyond the
present.

---

## Axiom 7

Learning modifies projections.

It never modifies history.

---

## Axiom 8

The Earth is a continuously evolving system.

Zoryn continuously updates its interpretation of that
system.

---

## Axiom 9

Every conclusion is traceable.

Every projection must identify the facts that produced it.

---

## Axiom 10

Everything is replaceable except the fact stream.
