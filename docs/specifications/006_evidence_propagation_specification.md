# Evidence Propagation Specification

## Purpose

This specification defines how evidence propagates through the
Dynamic Process Network.

Rather than propagating predictions, Zoryn propagates evidence.

Evidence continuously modifies confidence in processes,
interactions, explanations and theories.

---

# Philosophy

Evidence is local.

Understanding is global.

A single observation may influence distant parts of the Process
Network through chains of evidence.

---

# Principle

Every new fact contributes evidence.

Evidence propagates through connected processes.

Propagation modifies confidence.

It never modifies historical facts.

---

# Evidence Flow

Fact

↓

Evidence

↓

Process

↓

Interaction

↓

Explanation

↓

Theory

↓

Forecast

Every level accumulates evidence.

---

# Local Propagation

Evidence first affects directly connected processes.

Local updates occur immediately.

---

# Regional Propagation

Changes in one process influence neighbouring processes through
their interactions.

Influence decreases with network distance.

---

# Global Propagation

Large accumulations of evidence may eventually modify entire
theories.

Global revisions occur gradually.

---

# Positive Evidence

Positive evidence

strengthens

supports

confirms

existing explanations.

Confidence increases.

---

# Negative Evidence

Negative evidence

weakens

contradicts

or rejects

existing explanations.

Confidence decreases.

---

# Competing Evidence

Different explanations may receive evidence simultaneously.

Evidence therefore competes.

The strongest explanation is not necessarily the oldest.

---

# Evidence Decay

Evidence does not disappear.

Its influence may diminish as

better evidence,

more recent evidence,

or more complete evidence

becomes available.

History remains preserved.

---

# Evidence Accumulation

Confidence emerges from accumulated evidence.

No single observation should dominate understanding.

Repeated independent evidence produces robust knowledge.

---

# Uncertainty

Propagation preserves uncertainty.

Confidence must never exceed available evidence.

Unknowns remain explicit.

---

# Learning

Every propagation step may

strengthen a process,

weaken a process,

discover an interaction,

modify an explanation,

refine a theory,

generate a hypothesis.

Learning therefore occurs continuously.

---

# Constraints

Evidence never alters facts.

Evidence always preserves provenance.

Evidence propagation is reproducible.

Propagation remains explainable.

Every confidence update is traceable.

---

# Design Principles

1. Evidence propagates.
2. Facts do not.
3. Confidence emerges from evidence.
4. Evidence competes.
5. Uncertainty is preserved.
6. Learning is continual.
7. Every update is traceable.
8. Understanding improves through propagation.
