### **01 — Observation**

Authoritative datasets provide measurements for a place and time.
*Example: monthly rainfall = 420 mm.*

### **02 — Environmental Signature**

Observations are transformed into indicators describing environmental conditions.
*Example: rainfall → SPI = −1.4.*

### **03 — Relationship**

The knowledge graph connects observations, signatures, rules, and hypotheses.
*Example: SPI −1.4 → unusually dry condition.*

### **04 — Evidence**

Rules evaluate these relationships and produce evidence for or against hypotheses.
*Example: SPI below the defined threshold → evidence of dryness.*

### **05 — Pattern**

Related evidence is grouped into meaningful, repeatable patterns.
*Example: SPI declining and remaining low across several months → persistent drying pattern.*

### **06 — Hypothesis**

Patterns are evaluated against scientifically defined hypotheses.
*Example: persistent drying patterns → “Developing Meteorological Drought.”*

### **07 — Discovery**

When the required conditions are satisfied, Zoryn produces a deterministic discovery with supporting evidence and confidence.
*Example: “Developing Meteorological Drought” supported by 92 evidence items and 28 patterns.*

### **08 — Decision**

The discovery, evidence, confidence, and provenance are packaged into an interpretable output for decision-making.
*Example: an early-warning insight showing where drought is developing and why.*
