# Core Concepts

## Observation

A single measurement of the Earth at a specific place and time.

Examples:

- Rainfall
- Temperature
- NDVI
- Soil moisture
- River discharge
- Population movement
- Reservoir level

Observations are immutable.

---

## Entity

A real-world object.

Examples:

- H3 cell
- River
- Lake
- Road
- Settlement
- Forest
- Weather station

Entities persist through time.

---

## State

The condition of an entity at a particular instant.

A state is produced from one or more observations.

States evolve continuously.

---

## Event

A meaningful transition between states.

Examples:

- Vegetation collapse
- Reservoir depletion
- Sudden warming
- Crop recovery

Events are discovered—not necessarily predefined.

---

## Relationship

A connection between two entities.

Relationships may be:

- Spatial
- Temporal
- Hydrological
- Atmospheric
- Ecological
- Human
- Statistical
- Learned

Relationships evolve.

---

## Knowledge Graph

The complete representation of Earth's evolving system.

Nodes represent entities.

Edges represent relationships.

Node and edge properties evolve through time.

---

## Memory

Compressed knowledge extracted from historical graph evolution.

Memory is persistent.

Learning updates memory rather than replacing it.

---

## Pattern

A recurring sequence of graph states.

Patterns may be:

- Known
- Emerging
- Unknown

Patterns are independent of hazard labels.

---

## Prediction

A forecast of future graph evolution.

Hazards, impacts and risks emerge from predicted graph states.

---

## Explanation

The evidence supporting a prediction.

Every prediction should be traceable through graph evolution.
