# Measurement Spaces

## Purpose

This document defines the mathematical spaces in which scientific quantities exist.

Every measurable quantity within Zoryn belongs to a measurement space.

Measurement spaces provide the mathematical foundation for comparison, aggregation, propagation and learning.

---

## Philosophy

Measurements cannot exist without context.

Rainfall cannot be compared directly with confidence.

Temperature cannot be added to evidence.

Every scientific quantity belongs to its own mathematical space.

Operations are only valid within compatible spaces.

---

## Definition

A measurement space is a mathematical domain in which quantities of the same scientific meaning can be represented and manipulated consistently.

Each space defines

its quantities,

its units,

its allowable operations,

and its interpretation.

---

## Physical Space

Represents measurable properties of the Earth.

Examples include

Temperature

Rainfall

Pressure

Elevation

Discharge

Physical spaces follow internationally accepted units.

---

## Biological Space

Represents measurable biological properties.

Examples include

Biomass

Leaf Area Index

Species Richness

Primary Productivity

Biological spaces may contain both observed and derived quantities.

---

## Human System Space

Represents measurable human processes.

Examples include

Population

Migration

Food Security

Economic Activity

Infrastructure Capacity

Human system quantities evolve continuously.

---

## Knowledge Space

Represents scientific understanding.

Examples include

Information

Evidence

Confidence

Novelty

Theory Quality

Knowledge spaces are unique to scientific reasoning.

---

## Network Space

Represents relationships between processes.

Examples include

Interaction Strength

Connectivity

Influence

Propagation Distance

Feedback Strength

These quantities describe the Dynamic Process Network itself.

---

## State Space

Every system occupies a state within a state space.

Processes move systems through state space.

Forecasting projects future trajectories through state space.

---

## Theory Space

Scientific theories occupy a theory space.

Theory revision becomes movement through this space as understanding improves.

Theory space evolves continuously.

---

## Compatibility

Quantities may only be combined when mathematically compatible.

Examples

Temperature may be averaged.

Confidence may be accumulated.

Evidence may be propagated.

Rainfall cannot be added to confidence.

Scientific meaning determines mathematical validity.

---

## Transformations

Some quantities may be transformed between spaces.

Examples include

Observations becoming Information.

Information becoming Evidence.

Evidence influencing Confidence.

Confidence strengthening Theories.

Every transformation preserves scientific meaning.

---

## Design Principles

1. Every quantity belongs to a measurement space.
2. Measurement spaces define valid mathematical operations.
3. Scientific meaning determines compatibility.
4. Transformations preserve meaning.
5. Different spaces require different units.
6. State spaces describe system evolution.
7. Theory spaces describe scientific evolution.
8. Measurement spaces underpin all mathematical reasoning.
