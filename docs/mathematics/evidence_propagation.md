# Evidence Propagation

## Purpose

Evidence Propagation is the fundamental computational mechanism of Zoryn.

Its purpose is to continually update scientific understanding as
new evidence arrives.

Unlike traditional machine learning systems, Zoryn does not primarily
optimize predictions.

It continually updates explanations.

---

# Philosophy

Every observation contains information.

Information changes evidence.

Evidence changes confidence.

Confidence changes understanding.

Understanding changes future predictions.

Prediction is therefore the consequence of evidence propagation.

---

# Dynamic Process Network

The Earth is represented as

DPN = (P, I, E, T)

where

P = Processes

I = Interactions

E = Evidence

T = Time

Every process possesses

state,

confidence,

uncertainty,

history,

and evidence.

---

# Arrival of a New Fact

Every new fact initiates an Evidence Propagation Cycle.

Fact

↓

Validation

↓

Evidence Update

↓

Local Propagation

↓

Network Propagation

↓

Theory Update

↓

Forecast Update

↓

Wait for next fact

The system never stops evolving.

---

# Local Update

Suppose

Fi

is a newly received fact.

The evidence associated with the directly related process

Pi

is updated first.

Only local evidence changes immediately.

---

# Interaction Update

Every neighbouring process

Pj

receives propagated evidence through its interaction.

The strength of propagation depends upon

interaction confidence,

interaction strength,

temporal distance,

spatial distance,

historical consistency,

and evidence quality.

---

# Evidence Conservation

Evidence is never destroyed.

New evidence is accumulated.

Old evidence remains traceable.

Confidence changes.

History does not.

---

# Evidence Accumulation

Every process maintains

Supporting Evidence

Contradictory Evidence

Unknown Evidence

These evolve independently.

Scientific uncertainty is preserved.

---

# Propagation Distance

Evidence weakens with propagation distance.

Direct evidence is stronger than indirect evidence.

Long propagation chains increase uncertainty.

---

# Multiple Evidence Paths

Evidence may reach the same process through multiple paths.

Independent agreement strengthens confidence.

Independent contradiction weakens confidence.

Propagation therefore behaves like scientific consensus rather than voting.

---

# Feedback

Evidence may return to previously visited processes.

Feedback loops are expected.

Repeated reinforcement increases confidence only if supported by independent evidence.

Circular reasoning is prohibited.

---

# Theory Revision

When accumulated evidence exceeds predefined thresholds,

candidate explanations,

candidate processes,

or candidate theories

are revised.

Revision creates new facts.

History is preserved.

---

# Novelty

If evidence repeatedly fails to explain observations,

Novelty increases.

Novelty initiates

new hypotheses,

new candidate processes,

or new theories.

Failure therefore becomes productive.

---

# Continuous Learning

The propagation cycle never terminates.

Every incoming fact modifies

local understanding,

regional understanding,

global understanding,

and theoretical understanding.

Learning is continual.

---

# Design Principles

Evidence propagates.

Confidence propagates.

Facts do not propagate.

History never changes.

Understanding continuously evolves.
