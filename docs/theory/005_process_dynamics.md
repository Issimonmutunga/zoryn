# Process Dynamics

## Purpose

This document defines how processes evolve within Zoryn.

Processes are not static.

They emerge, evolve, interact and sometimes disappear.

Understanding process dynamics is fundamental to understanding the Earth.

---

## Philosophy

The Earth is continuously changing.

Processes are the mechanisms responsible for this change.

A process is not an event.

It is an evolving mechanism that produces state transitions through time.

---

## Definition

Process Dynamics describes the evolution of processes and their interactions across space and time.

Every process possesses behaviour.

Behaviour changes.

Therefore processes evolve.

---

## Process Lifecycle

Every process progresses through a lifecycle.

Candidate

↓

Emerging

↓

Active

↓

Stable

↓

Weakening

↓

Inactive

↓

Archived

A process may return to an earlier stage if new evidence appears.

---

## Process Behaviour

Every process possesses

Direction

Magnitude

Persistence

Velocity

Variability

Uncertainty

Behaviour evolves continuously.

---

## Process Evolution

Processes may

strengthen,

weaken,

accelerate,

slow,

split,

merge,

stabilize,

or disappear.

Evolution is continuous.

---

## Process Interactions

Processes rarely evolve independently.

Processes may

reinforce,

oppose,

delay,

trigger,

or synchronize

other processes.

Interactions themselves evolve.

---

## Spatial Dynamics

Processes operate across multiple spatial scales.

Local

↓

Regional

↓

Continental

↓

Global

Changes at one scale may influence another.

---

## Temporal Dynamics

Processes evolve over different time scales.

Examples include

Seconds

Hours

Days

Months

Years

Centuries

Different processes may interact despite operating on different temporal scales.

---

## Stability

Processes exhibit different forms of stability.

Stable

Oscillatory

Adaptive

Critical

Chaotic

Stable processes are not necessarily inactive.

---

## Tipping Points

Continuous evolution may eventually produce abrupt transitions.

These transitions are known as tipping points.

A tipping point represents a qualitative change in process behaviour.

Early detection is a primary objective of Zoryn.

---

## Emergence

Interactions among evolving processes may generate entirely new behaviour.

These emergent processes become candidates for scientific investigation.

Discovery is therefore an inherent consequence of process dynamics.

---

## Learning

Every new fact may modify

process behaviour,

interaction strength,

process confidence,

or process maturity.

The Dynamic Process Network therefore evolves continuously.

---

## Design Principles

1. Processes evolve continuously.
2. Behaviour is dynamic.
3. Processes interact across scales.
4. Stability is observable.
5. Tipping points emerge naturally.
6. New processes may emerge.
7. Learning never stops.
8. Process dynamics drive Earth system evolution.
