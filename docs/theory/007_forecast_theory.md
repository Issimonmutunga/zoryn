# Forecast Theory

## Purpose

This document defines forecasting within Zoryn.

Forecasting is not the prediction of isolated variables.

Forecasting is the projection of future system evolution based upon current scientific understanding.

---

## Philosophy

The future is not observed.

It is inferred.

Forecasts are not facts.

They are scientific hypotheses describing plausible future system states.

Forecasts evolve as understanding improves.

---

## Definition

A forecast is a scientifically justified projection of one or more future system states.

Forecasts are generated from

current system states,

known processes,

process interactions,

causal relationships,

and available evidence.

---

## Scientific Perspective

Forecasting is a consequence of understanding.

Better theories produce better forecasts.

Poor understanding produces uncertain forecasts.

Forecast quality therefore depends upon scientific understanding rather than computational complexity.

---

## Forecast Hierarchy

            Current System

                 ↓

            Current State

                 ↓

              Processes

                 ↓

            Interactions

                 ↓

              Evidence

                 ↓

          Scientific Theory

                 ↓

          Future Projection

                 ↓

              Forecast

Forecasts emerge from accumulated understanding.

---

## Types of Forecasts

Zoryn recognizes several forms of forecasting.

State Forecast

Projects future system states.

---

Process Forecast

Projects future process evolution.

---

Interaction Forecast

Projects changing interactions between processes.

---

Emergence Forecast

Projects the appearance of new system behaviour.

---

Theory Forecast

Projects how scientific understanding itself may evolve.

---

## Forecast Horizon

Forecasts may describe

- Minutes

- Hours

- Days

- Weeks

- Months

- Years

- Decades

Longer horizons generally possess greater uncertainty.

---

## Forecast Uncertainty

Every forecast contains uncertainty.

Uncertainty reflects

- incomplete evidence,

- incomplete theories,

- unknown processes,

- unexpected interactions,

- future observations not yet available.

Uncertainty is never hidden.

---

## Scenario Forecasting

Different assumptions produce different futures.

Zoryn therefore supports multiple concurrent scenarios.

No single scenario is assumed to be correct.

Scientific comparison is encouraged.

---

## Forecast Revision

Forecasts evolve continuously.

Every new observation may

strengthen,

weaken,

extend,

or invalidate

existing forecasts.

Revision creates new forecasts.

Historical forecasts remain preserved.

---

## Forecast Evaluation

Forecasts are evaluated by

scientific consistency,

agreement with observations,

predictive performance,

explanatory power,

and evidence support.

Forecast evaluation improves future forecasting.

---

## Learning

Forecast failures are scientifically valuable.

Unexpected outcomes improve

process models,

causal understanding,

interaction models,

and scientific theories.

Learning therefore continues after every forecast.

---

## Design Principles

1. Forecasts are hypotheses about future states.
2. Forecasts emerge from scientific understanding.
3. Every forecast contains uncertainty.
4. Multiple futures are expected.
5. Forecasts evolve continuously.
6. Historical forecasts are preserved.
7. Forecast failures improve understanding.
8. Forecasting is a consequence of learning.

---
