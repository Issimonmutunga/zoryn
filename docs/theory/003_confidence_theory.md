# Confidence Theory
## Purpose

This document defines confidence within Zoryn.

Confidence measures the degree to which current scientific understanding is supported by available evidence.

Confidence is dynamic.

It evolves continuously as new evidence becomes available.

## Philosophy

Scientific knowledge is rarely certain.

Confidence reflects the current strength of scientific support.

Confidence is not belief.

Confidence is not certainty.

Confidence represents the present state of understanding.

## Definition

Confidence is the degree to which available evidence supports an explanation, process or theory.

Confidence increases through supporting evidence.

Confidence decreases through contradictory evidence.

Confidence never becomes absolute.

## Scientific Perspective

Confidence belongs to knowledge.

It does not belong to facts.

Facts exist independently.

Confidence reflects how well facts support scientific understanding.

## Confidence Hierarchy

                                Fact

                                 ↓

                             Information

                                 ↓

                              Evidence

                                 ↓

                             Confidence

                                 ↓

                             Explanation

                                 ↓

                               Theory

Confidence emerges from accumulated evidence.

## Sources of Confidence

Confidence is influenced by

Evidence quality

Evidence quantity

Evidence independence

Temporal consistency

Spatial consistency

Predictive performance

Scientific reproducibility

No single factor determines confidence.

## Confidence Evolution

Confidence may

increase,

decrease,

stabilize,

oscillate,

or remain uncertain.

Every new fact may alter confidence.

Learning never stops.

## Contradictory Evidence

Contradictory evidence is expected.

Contradiction does not necessarily invalidate a theory.

Instead it initiates scientific revision.

Confidence therefore evolves gradually.

## Confidence and Novelty

Highly novel observations may initially possess low confidence.

As independent evidence accumulates,

confidence increases.

Discovery therefore precedes certainty.

## Confidence and Forecasting

Forecast confidence depends upon

the confidence of supporting processes,

the confidence of interactions,

the confidence of explanations,

and the confidence of underlying theories.

Forecast confidence is inherited.

## Confidence Preservation

Historical confidence is never overwritten.

As theories evolve,

confidence histories remain available for scientific analysis.

Understanding therefore remains reproducible.

## Design Principles

1. Confidence is evidence-driven.
2. Confidence evolves continuously.
3. Confidence is never absolute.
4. Contradictory evidence is valuable.
5. Confidence belongs to scientific understanding.
6. Historical confidence is preserved.
7. Forecasts inherit confidence.
8. Scientific uncertainty remains explicit.
