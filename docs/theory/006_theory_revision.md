# Theory Revision

## Purpose

This document defines how scientific theories evolve within Zoryn.

Scientific theories are not static.

They improve continuously as new evidence becomes available.

Theory revision is therefore a fundamental capability of Zoryn.

---

## Philosophy

Science advances through continual revision.

No theory is final.

Every theory remains open to refinement, extension or replacement.

Revision is evidence-driven.

---

## Definition

Theory Revision is the continual process of improving scientific understanding by incorporating new evidence while preserving historical knowledge.

Revision modifies understanding.

It never rewrites history.

---

## Theory Lifecycle

Every theory progresses through a lifecycle.

               Candidate

                  ↓

               Emerging

                  ↓

              Supported

                  ↓

             Established

                  ↓

               Revised

                 ↓

             Deprecated

                 ↓

              Archived

Historical theories remain part of scientific knowledge.

---

## Revision Triggers

Theory revision may be initiated by

New observations

Contradictory evidence

Novel processes

Unexpected interactions

Improved explanations

Independent validation

Repeated forecasting failures

No single trigger is sufficient on its own.

---

## Types of Revision

Theories may

Strengthen

Weaken

Expand

Simplify

Merge

Split

Be Replaced

Each represents scientific progress.

---

## Competing Theories

Multiple theories may explain the same observations.

Competing theories coexist until evidence favours one or more explanations.

Zoryn does not assume a single correct theory.

---

## Preservation of History

Previous theories are never deleted.

Every revision preserves

its evidence,

its assumptions,

its confidence,

its historical context.

Scientific evolution remains reproducible.

---

## Scientific Consistency

A revised theory should

explain more observations,

reduce unnecessary assumptions,

remain internally consistent,

remain compatible with established evidence,

or explicitly justify disagreement.

Revision should improve understanding.

---

## Theory Quality

Theory quality depends upon

Explanatory Power

Predictive Performance

Evidence Support

Reproducibility

Generality

Simplicity

Scientific quality evolves through time.

---

## Discovery

Theory revision may produce

new hypotheses,

new processes,

new causal pathways,

or entirely new theories.

Revision is therefore a mechanism of discovery.

---

## Learning

Every revision contributes to

better explanations,

better forecasts,

better decisions,

and deeper scientific understanding.

Learning is cumulative.

---

## Design Principles

1. No theory is final.
2. Revision is evidence-driven.
3. Historical theories are preserved.
4. Competing theories are expected.
5. Revision improves understanding.
6. Contradictory evidence is valuable.
7. Discovery emerges through revision.
8. Scientific knowledge evolves continuously.
