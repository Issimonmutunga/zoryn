# Standards Glossary

This glossary defines the canonical terminology used throughout Zoryn.

The purpose of this document is to ensure that documentation, code, validation reports, and scientific rules use consistent language.

Where possible, terminology follows internationally accepted environmental and disaster risk practice.

---

# Observation

A single measured or derived environmental variable for one H3 cell at one timestamp.

Examples

- precipitation_amount
- air_temperature
- soil_moisture

Observations are the atomic inputs to the reasoning engine.

---

# Signature

The complete environmental state of one H3 cell at one timestamp.

A Signature is an immutable collection of observations representing the environment at a specific point in time.

---

# Relationship

A comparison between two consecutive Signatures.

Relationships describe environmental change.

Examples include

- rainfall increased
- temperature decreased
- vegetation remained unchanged

Relationships contain no interpretation.

---

# Evidence

An immutable fact extracted from a Relationship.

Evidence represents meaningful environmental change that may support future reasoning.

Evidence is the primary input to PatternRules.

---

# Pattern

A recurring or persistent behaviour discovered from multiple Evidence objects.

Patterns describe environmental behaviour through time.

Examples

- persistent drying
- repeated heavy rainfall
- seasonal vegetation decline

Patterns do not infer causes.

---

# Pattern Rule

A scientific algorithm that discovers Patterns from Evidence.

Each PatternRule should be traceable to recognised scientific guidance whenever possible.

---

# Hypothesis

A candidate explanation for one or more Patterns.

Examples

- drought developing
- heatwave emerging
- flood conditions increasing

Hypotheses represent interpreted environmental states.

---

# Discovery

A validated Hypothesis supported by sufficient evidence and reasoning.

Discoveries become reusable environmental knowledge.

---

# Knowledge

Environmental understanding accumulated through repeated Discoveries.

Knowledge represents persistent understanding rather than individual events.

---

# Warning

An actionable notification generated from validated environmental knowledge.

Warnings communicate elevated environmental risk to downstream systems or users.

---

# Hazard

A potentially damaging environmental process or event.

Examples

- drought
- flood
- wildfire
- cyclone
- heatwave

---

# Exposure

People, infrastructure, ecosystems, or assets that may be affected by a Hazard.

Exposure does not imply impact.

---

# Vulnerability

The susceptibility of exposed systems to damage from a Hazard.

Vulnerability depends on environmental, physical, economic, and social factors.

---

# Risk

The combination of

- Hazard
- Exposure
- Vulnerability

Risk represents the likelihood of adverse consequences.

---

# Early Warning

The timely identification and communication of increasing environmental risk before damaging impacts occur.

Early warning is the ultimate objective of the Zoryn reasoning engine.

---

# Confidence

A quantitative measure describing the degree of support for a Pattern, Hypothesis, Discovery, or Warning.

Confidence should be derived from evidence and scientific methodology rather than subjective judgement.

---

# Traceability

The ability to identify

- observations
- evidence
- reasoning rules
- scientific standards

that contributed to any conclusion produced by Zoryn.

Every reasoning product generated by Zoryn should be fully traceable.

---

# Explainability

The ability of Zoryn to communicate how and why a conclusion was reached.

Explainability is a core design principle of the reasoning engine.

---

# Scientific Defensibility

The principle that every environmental conclusion should be supported by accepted scientific methodology, operational guidance, or peer-reviewed literature.

Scientific defensibility is a primary design objective of Zoryn.
