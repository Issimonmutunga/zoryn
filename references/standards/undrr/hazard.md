# Scope

This document defines the concepts of hazard, disaster risk reduction,
and multi-hazard early warning used throughout Zoryn.

It provides the conceptual framework for translating scientific
environmental reasoning into actionable warnings.

---

# Objectives

Zoryn should

- detect hazards
- monitor hazard evolution
- assess environmental risk
- communicate increasing risk before impacts occur

The objective is early action rather than post-event analysis.

---

# Hazard

A process, phenomenon or human activity that may cause

- loss of life
- injury
- environmental degradation
- economic loss
- disruption

Hazards may be

- meteorological
- hydrological
- climatological
- biological
- geological

Zoryn currently focuses on environmental hazards.

---

# Multi-Hazard

Hazards rarely occur in isolation.

Multiple hazards may

- occur simultaneously
- occur sequentially
- amplify one another

Examples

Heat

↓

Vegetation stress

↓

Wildfire

or

Persistent rainfall

↓

Soil saturation

↓

Flooding

Multi-hazard reasoning is a long-term objective of Zoryn.

---

# Early Warning

An effective early warning system should

1. Understand risk.

2. Monitor hazards.

3. Detect increasing danger.

4. Communicate warnings.

5. Support timely action.

These principles guide the architecture of the Zoryn reasoning engine.

# Hazard Evolution

Hazards evolve through time.

Examples include

- developing drought
- intensifying heatwave
- increasing flood potential
- recovering environmental conditions

Reasoning should therefore operate on environmental history rather than
isolated observations.

---

# Design Principles

Hazards should be

- observable
- explainable
- reproducible
- scientifically defensible
- traceable

Every warning should be explainable from

Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Pattern

Scientific Standard
